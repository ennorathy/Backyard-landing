01 Business model

How our
model works.

Localization for one language market is often the first step: a rights holder licenses us that market, sees the results, and opens the title to YouTube in more markets and territories.

01LicenseTypically exclusive in the core territory of the language market (e.g. MENA for Arabic), non-exclusive worldwide.
02Fund localizationAt our cost — the rights holder pays nothing.
03Release & protectProgrammed and monetized on our channels; rights protected in-house.
04Analyze demandWatch-time data drives the next acquisitions.
Who benefits from the model
YouTubeLicensed content new to the platform — localized by us for new language markets, and in its original language.
Rights holdersNew markets, revenue and data on where their content is in demand.
ViewersFilms that were paywalled, subscription-only or never available in their language — now legal and free.

Why we manage rights ourselves
01
Exclusive licensee
Most of our 2,000+ assets are licensed to us exclusively for defined language markets. Protecting them is not only our right as exclusive licensee, but our contractual responsibility to the rights holder.
02
Direct accountability
We report directly to our 30+ partners. In-house rights protection, transparent analytics and next-month royalties are how we earn the trust of rights holders.
03
Trust brings new content
Many of our partners put their films on YouTube for the first time. Direct, transparent work is the only way to bring them to the platform.
